╔══════════════════════════════════════════╗
║         How to start JobForge           ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════╝

macOS:
  1. Right-click "JobForge.command"
  2. Click "Open"
  3. Click "Open" again in the security popup
     (only needed the very first time)
  4. A Terminal window opens and sets everything up.
     Your browser opens automatically when it's ready.

  Next time: just double-click JobForge.command

Windows:
  1. Double-click "JobForge.bat"
  2. If Windows Defender pops up, click "More info" → "Run anyway"
     (only needed the very first time)
  3. A window opens and sets everything up.
     Your browser opens automatically when it's ready.

  Next time: just double-click JobForge.bat
